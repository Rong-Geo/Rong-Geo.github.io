A dataset worth knowing

Selected from the awesome-datascience list of open data sets on GitHub, hosted within the AWS Registry of Open Data.

Common Crawl

Registry of Open Data on AWS — registry.opendata.aws/commoncrawl

Common Crawl is an open archive of web-page content, text extracts, and metadata gathered from billions of pages, refreshed with a new snapshot roughly every month since 2008. Its scale and openness make it a common foundation for training natural-language-processing systems, for tracking how information changes across the web over time, and for identifying data that has been unintentionally exposed on public sites. I chose it because it shows, very concretely, what "volume" and "velocity" mean in Big Data — a dataset too large for one machine, growing every month.

A book worth reading

Selected from the free-data-science-books list on GitHub.

Introduction to Information Retrieval

Christopher D. Manning & Hinrich Schütze (Stanford University), Prabhakar Raghavan — Cambridge University Press — nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book

The book moves from foundational ideas — indexing, Boolean search — toward statistical ranking and web-scale retrieval, with worked examples throughout. I am drawn to it because so much of Big Data work is really about search: finding the relevant fraction of a massive, unstructured collection such as Common Crawl, quickly and accurately.
